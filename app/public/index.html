How it works

From paper to blocks in four steps

01

Upload your plan

A photo, a scan or a PDF. One page per floor is perfect.

02

Claude reads it

Rooms, labels, dimensions, doors, windows, stairs and open courtyards, each marked "from the plan" or "guessed".

03

Answer a few questions

Everything found on the plan is already filled in. You confirm the style, who sleeps where, what is open to the sky.

04

Move into the city

Your house gets a plot on the city streets, next to the others. Everyone visiting sees each other.

Controls

Phone: it opens full screen  ·  left thumb to walk, right thumb to look  ·  buttons to jump, fly and chat
